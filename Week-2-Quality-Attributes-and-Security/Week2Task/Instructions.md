# Week 2 Lab: Quality Attributes and Threat Modelling

## The Scenario
You are the architect for a new online banking application.

## Your Task
1. Write three precise quality attribute scenarios (for example, performance, availability, and security). Use the formal structure: Source, Stimulus, Environment, Artefact, Response, and Response Measure.
2. Create a simple threat model table listing three valuable assets, the threats against them, and the architectural mitigation.
