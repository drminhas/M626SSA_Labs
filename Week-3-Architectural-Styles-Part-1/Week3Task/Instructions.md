# Week 3 Lab: Architectural Styles Part 1

## The Scenario
Choose a popular application you use daily, such as Spotify, Netflix, or a local university portal.

## Your Task
1. Identify which architectural style (Layered, Client Server, Pipe and Filter, or MVC) best describes this application.
2. Draw a comparison matrix comparing your chosen style against the Layered architecture. Score them on maintainability and performance.
